# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

To report a security issue, please use the GitHub Security Advisory "[Report a Vulnerability](https://github.com/pauljherring/g2ttrss-mobile/security/advisories/new)" tab.

You should receive a response indicating the next steps in handling your report within a couple of days. 
After the initial reply to your report, you will be kept informed of the progress towards a fix and
full announcement, and may ask for additional information or guidance.
